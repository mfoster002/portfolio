Thrift Finder

Project Overview

Thrift Finder is an app designed to help users locate thrift shops
in their area. The app features a user-friendly interface, search
filters, and personalized recommendations to make thrift shopping
more accessible and enjoyable.

Key Features

  • Thrift shop locator with store details (hours, contact info)
  • User reviews and ratings with sorting options
  • Interactive map integration for directions
  • User profiles to track reviews and favorites
  • Enhanced accessibility with larger buttons and text spacing

Problem & Goal

Problem: Many users struggle to find thrift shops that
match their preferences and budget.
Goal: Provide a platform that simplifies the process of
finding thrift shops and enhances the shopping experience.

Lo-Fi Protype

User Research & Insights

Methods: Surveys, interviews, competitive analysis

Main Pain Points:

  • Lack of centralized information about thrift shops
  • Difficulty filtering shops by category or price
  • Limited options for personalized recommendations

Case Study

User Testing

Survey on Desired Features:
- 43% of users wanted store hours and phone numbers directly in the app.
Design Change: Included store contact info and improved visibility.

Preference Testing:
- 70% preferred a thicker font for headers.
- 80% preferred rounded shapes.
Design Change: Adopted thicker fonts and rounded buttons.

Maze Unmoderated Study:
- Users could find the "Full Hours" button, but the text was too small.
Design Change: Increased text size, enhanced color and font weight, and improved spacing.

2nd Usability Study (Moderated):
- Users needed a sort button for reviews.
- Trouble finding additional store hours.
Design Change: Added a sort button, increased spacing, and used bold, capitalized fonts for clarity.
