Pillzy

Project Overview

Pillzy is a medication management mobile app designed to help users aged 18-24
manage their medications and health. The app features a user-friendly interface,
personalized medication reminders, and a gamified user experience to promote
adherence and understanding of medications.

Key Features

  • Personalized medication reminders
  • Gamified user experience
  • Medication tracking and management

User Persona

Developing a User Persona Made the Problem Clear

Managing medications isn’t always easy—especially when life is busy.
People forget doses, get overwhelmed by complex schedules, or drop off
apps that ask for too much. I wanted to explore how design could step in
and ease that experience with empathy, and simplicity.

Key Takeaways

  • Users want a simple, intuitive interface that makes it easy to manage
    their medications.
  • Clear visuals (like color or icons) help avoid confusion.

Wireframes

These wireframes were created to outline the app's layout and functionality.

I focused on creating a clean, user-friendly interface that prioritized ease of use.

User Testing

After conducting user testing, I received valuable feedback that helped refine the design.

Users appreciated the simplicity of the interface but suggested adding more visual cues to enhance usability.

Final Design

The final design incorporates user feedback and focuses on a clean, modern aesthetic.

It features a color palette that is both calming and engaging, with clear typography for easy readability.
