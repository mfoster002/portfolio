ABOUT

Hi, I'm Marian!

I'm a web and UX design student based in Charlotte, NC,
graduating from Wake Tech in Spring 2025. My creative journey began
with a Bachelor's in Mass Communications, where I discovered my
love for visual storytelling and thoughtful design.

Whether in class or collaborating on freelance projects, I love
bringing ideas to life—designing posters, business cards, and album
covers for friends and small businesses. I'm especially inspired by
bold color palettes, strong typography, and work that makes people
feel something.

Outside of design, you can usually find me reading, discovering
hidden gems while thrift shopping, or cheering from the stands at a
basketball game.
