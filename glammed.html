Glammed

Project Overview

Glammed explores how beauty tech can be more inclusive, accessible, and empowering. Designed for users of all genders and skill levels, it’s a space to learn, experiment, and express style—your way. With a clean interface and flexible tutorials, Glammed puts the focus on individuality over perfection.

Key Features

  • Inclusive Tutorials: Video and text tutorials designed for all genders and learning styles.
  • Accessibility Enhancements: High-contrast designs and captions for inclusivity.
  • Customizable Learning Paths: Choose tutorials that match your pace and preferences.

Problem & Goal

Problem:

Users often feel overwhelmed by generic tutorials and products that don’t align with their unique features or learning styles.

Goal:

Create a platform that offers inclusive tutorials and accessible features, making beauty exploration empowering for everyone.

User Research & Insights

Methods:

  • Competitive audits
  • Personas
  • Empathy & journey maps

Main Pain Points:

  • Overwhelming amount of information without guidance.
  • Tutorials not matching individual learning styles.
  • Lack of inclusivity in tutorials and product recommendations.

Wireframes

These wireframes focused on creating an intuitive layout with clear navigation and engaging visuals.

Tutorials were designed to include both video and text options for accessibility, with expandable menus and side navigation for ease of use.

User Testing

Designing Glammed was a collaborative process, shaped by user feedback at every stage. Testing revealed opportunities to improve navigation, enhance tutorial accessibility, and make the app more inclusive.

What I Learned:

Users valued the inclusive tutorials but wanted clearer navigation and more flexible learning options.

Key Design Changes:

  • Improved navigation with a dedicated tutorial library.
  • Enhanced accessibility with high-contrast colors and captions.
  • Added customizable learning paths for a more personalized experience.

Final Design

The final design of Glammed is a reflection of the feedback and insights gathered throughout the design process.

By incorporating inclusive tutorials, high-contrast visuals, and a calming color palette, the design ensures accessibility and usability for all.
